# Scoped project rules

- Paper universe = top 20 perps by volume + always-on coins + `desk_config.paper_extra_coins`. Extras come only from `/api/cron/desk-universe` (`universe.server.ts`): it backfills ranks 21–40, backtests them as a group (rules in pure `universe.ts`, tests in `universe.test.ts`) and writes the coins that pass. Risk-blocked approved signals become shadow positions with `shadow_reason = 'risk'`; vetoed ones get `'vetoed'`. Why: more practice trades on paper without touching live, and veto value stays measurable.
- Practice follows: `rebuildWatchlist` adds up to 20 swing traders (score ≥ 3) below the A/B bar (`sm_scores.practice`); their opens become `practice_follow` signals (paper only, never in consensus counts, hard-blocked in `liveEntryBlock`). The grading queue serves watched wallets, then Invo traders (originators first), then the leaderboard. Why: learn from Invo traders daily without touching real money.
- Scouted Invo traders: real-money Invo portfolios the owner asked to follow, matched to their Hyperliquid wallet (open positions and recent fills must line up), are tagged `'invo_scout'` in `sm_wallets.sources` with the Invo username in `display_name`. `scoutedAddresses()` (max `SCOUT_SLOTS` = 30) makes them practice wallets outside the 20 practice slots and always-mirrored Mimic wallets; their opens count from 0.5% of account value (`SCOUT_MIN_FRAC`) and carry `regime = 'invo_scout_open'`. Paper only. The match is done offline from the Invo site; app code never calls Invo or stores Invo credentials. Why: follow what proven Invo traders do, on-chain and credential-free.
- Invo bridge (`bridge/`, installed on the owner's Windows PC in Documents\APEX Invo Bridge): Node + playwright-core drives the installed Edge with its own profile, logged into Invo by the owner. Each round it opens every followed portfolio (in-app navigation, full load as fallback, scrolls for more) and posts every JSON response from `capture_hosts` to `/api/invo/ingest` (gzip, `x-bridge-secret`; APEX stores only the secret's SHA-256 in `app_private` 'invo_bridge_secret_sha256'). Targets, URLs, hosts and timing come from `/api/invo/targets`, so Invo changes are fixed in APEX, not on the PC. Parsing is content-based and pure (`invo-parse.ts`, tests in `invo-parse.test.ts`): positions are recognised by coin + side + entry price + open time, and closes are inferred only when certain (complete list, shown closed, or a newest-first page that covers the position). Followed portfolios are pseudo-wallets `invo:<portfolio id>` (sources 'invo_scout' + 'invo_bridge', display_name '<username> · note'), so the tracker, follow signals, follow exits and Mimic treat them like wallets; every open counts (no size floor). Unreadable visits are kept in `invo_bridge_raw`; `invo_bridge_runs` is the health log, and the tracker warns in agent_logs ('INVO BRIDGE') when rounds stop or Invo looks logged out. The bridge restarts itself when the PC wakes from sleep or a round stops making progress (watchdog in `bridge.mjs`), cleans up browser processes left on its own profile before a launch, and Windows Task Scheduler runs `bridge.mjs --ensure` every 10 minutes (installed by `install-autostart.cmd`), which starts it unless a bridge with a fresh `heartbeat.txt` is running; `stop.cmd` writes `paused.flag` so that check leaves a manual stop alone. Why: follow paper and unmatched Invo traders directly from Invo, without APEX calling Invo's API or holding Invo credentials.
- Weekly Invo re-scout: the bridge opens `SCOUT_PAGES` (Invo leaderboard) about once a day and APEX keeps the raw responses in `invo_bridge_raw` ('scout:leaderboard'). The scheduled "APEX Invo re-scout" agent reads them plus Mimic results and changes the scouted list only through service_role-only `invo_follow_set(address, username, note)` / `invo_follow_drop(address)` (max 30, paper only). Why: the follow list refreshes itself, and traders are kept or dropped on APEX's own after-fee copy results rather than Invo's stats.
- Mimic simulator (`src/lib/sm/mimic.ts` pure + tests, `mimic.server.ts` I/O): up to 20 active, profitable Invo traders (`sm_scores.mirror`) get $100 virtual copies in `mimic_trades`, opened when `trackWatchlist` sees them open and closed when they close, after Invo fees. No review, no risk rules, never touches the paper desk or live. Why: measure whether copying Invo traders the way the video does pays after fees and delay.
