# Scoped project rules

- FX paper desk (`/fx`): two paper books in `fx_books` ('sprint', 'steady') on Hyperliquid xyz (HIP-3) perps. Paper only: nothing here signs or sends orders, and it never touches `desk_config` trading state, the crypto paper desk or live. Why: the owner wants to test Claude as an FX trader without risk.
- Claude trades only through service_role-only SQL functions `fx_open`, `fx_close`, `fx_amend` and reads `fx_context()`; every rule (book active, allowed coin, quote < 10 min, weekend window Fri 22:00–Sun 23:00 Stockholm, max open, one position per coin, stop side/distance, daily loss stop, $10 minimum) is enforced inside `fx_open`. Why: an external agent can't bypass the rules.
- Sizing, isolated liquidation price and the 1-minute bar walk are pure in `fx.ts` (tests in `fx.test.ts`); `fx_open` / `fx__close` repeat the sizing and close formulas in SQL, so change both together. Entry fee comes off `cash_usd` at open; a close adds net + entry fee; liquidation loses margin + fees. Why: one tested definition, applied atomically in the database.
- `/api/cron/fx-tick` (pg_cron 'fx-tick', every 5 min, `recordRun` key 'fx_engine') writes quotes, keeps 1h/1d candles for the five coins in `hl_candles`, walks open positions through `fx_settle` and writes hourly equity / busted status through `fx_snapshot`. Only the owner's `resetBookFn` restarts a busted book. Why: same cron-auth and run-logging pattern as the other desks.
